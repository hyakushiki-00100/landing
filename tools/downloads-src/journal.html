Journal Template

Daily Reflection

A simple template for a calmer start and end to the day

This is a one-page journal template — print it, or copy the layout into your own notebook, and use one page per day. It's designed to take two or three minutes in the morning and two or three minutes at night. Nothing more is required.

How to use it

Morning

Before you open your phone, name one intention for the day and one thing you're willing to let go of before you start. Keep both to a single line each — the goal is clarity, not a plan.

Evening

Before bed, write down what actually went well, and one thing to release before sleep. Add three short lines of gratitude — they don't need to be profound. "Coffee" is a perfectly good answer.

There's no wrong way to fill this in. One honest word is better than five polished sentences. If a section doesn't apply on a given day, leave it blank.
