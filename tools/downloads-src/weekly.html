The Weekly Check-In

A short guide to a weekly Zen practice

A day is too short to see a pattern. A week is just long enough. This short guide is a five-to-ten-minute practice for the end of the week — a quiet check-in on where your attention actually went, versus where you meant it to go.

When to use it

Sunday evening or Monday morning both work well — anywhere near the boundary between one week and the next. Find a quiet ten minutes, on paper if possible. Handwriting slows the thought down just enough to notice more of it.

How to use it

The next page has five short prompts. Answer them briefly — a sentence or two each is enough. This isn't a performance review. There's no target to hit and nothing to optimize. You're simply looking, honestly, at one ordinary week.

"You should sit in meditation for twenty minutes every day — unless you're too busy. Then you should sit for an hour."
